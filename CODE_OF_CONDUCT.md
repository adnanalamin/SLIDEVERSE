# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SLIDEVERSE to be an open, welcoming and respectful community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome beginners and people with different experience levels.
- Give feedback that focuses on the work, not the person.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Help keep discussions focused and safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, doxxing, threats, spam and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details publicly in an issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
